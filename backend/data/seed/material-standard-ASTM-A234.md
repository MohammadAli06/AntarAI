# Material Standard — ASTM A234 / IS 2062

## Piping Fittings and Structural Steel for Refinery Service

### 1. Scope
This standard covers the material requirements for carbon-steel piping fittings (ASTM A234 WPB) and structural steel (IS 2062) used in process piping and support structures within the refinery battery limits.

### 2. Chemical Composition
Carbon-steel fittings shall conform to the following ladle analysis: carbon 0.30% max, manganese 0.29-1.06%, phosphorus 0.05% max, sulfur 0.05% max, silicon 0.10% min. Each heat shall be traceable to a material certificate listing heat number, chemistry, and mechanical properties.

### 3. Mechanical Properties
Tensile strength shall be 415-585 MPa, yield strength 205 MPa minimum, elongation 16% minimum in 50 mm gauge length. Hardness shall not exceed 194 HBW for fittings intended for sour service.

### 4. Traceability
All fittings and structural members shall carry a heat-number stamp traceable to the material certificate. Fittings installed in sour service (NACE MR0175) require additional hardness verification and HIC testing certificates retained for the life of the asset.

### 5. Acceptance
Material acceptance requires the material certificate, visual inspection free of laps, cracks, and surface defects, and dimensional verification against the applicable schedule. Reinstatement of any piping spool requires verification that installed fittings carry the correct heat-number stamp matching the retained certificate.
