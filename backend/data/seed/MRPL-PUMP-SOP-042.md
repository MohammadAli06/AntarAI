# MRPL Standard Operating Procedure — SOP-PUMP-042

## Centrifugal Pump Vibration Monitoring and Reinstatement

**Document ID:** MRPL-PUMP-SOP-042
**Revision:** 7  **Effective:** 2026-01-01  **Owner:** Rotating Equipment Group

### 1. Scope
This procedure governs vibration monitoring, acceptance, and reinstatement of centrifugal pumps in the crude distillation unit (CDU) and vacuum gas oil unit (VGU) at the Mangalore Refinery.

### 2. Vibration Limits
Maximum allowable vibration for centrifugal pumps at rated speed shall not exceed **4.5 mm/s RMS** (velocity, unfiltered). Values exceeding this threshold require immediate inspection and are not acceptable for reinstatement without engineering review and a documented deviation.

### 3. Bearing Temperature
Bearing temperature must be recorded at startup and again after 30 minutes of continuous operation. A deviation of more than **15 °C** from the established baseline requires investigation. Steady-state bearing temperature shall not exceed **85 °C** for oil-lubricated bearings and **95 °C** for grease-lubricated bearings.

### 4. Reinstatement Checklist
Reinstatement of any pump following maintenance requires documented verification of all items in Appendix A, signed by the certifying engineer. Items include: alignment verification (within 0.05 mm), mechanical seal leak test, vibration within limits, bearing temperature within limits, and oil sample analysis report.

### 5. Deviation Handling
Any deviation from the limits in Section 2 or 3 must be recorded on form MRPL-DEV-001, reviewed by the area maintenance superintendent, and approved by the reliability engineer before the pump is returned to service.
