# Previous Approval Note #221

## Pump P-199 Reinstatement Following Vibration Exceedance

**Task:** TASK-221  **Date:** 2026-03-14  **Certifying Engineer:** R. Kulkarni

### Findings
Pump P-199 (CDU charge pump, 1 of 2) experienced a vibration exceedance event on 2026-03-12, reaching 5.8 mm/s RMS at the non-drive-end bearing housing (limit per SOP-042: 4.5 mm/s RMS). Root-cause analysis identified outer-race spalling on the non-drive-end bearing.

### Corrective Action
The non-drive-end bearing (SKF 6311 C3) was replaced with an approved-vendor-list bearing of identical specification. Internal clearance was verified at 0.045 mm, within the C3 tolerance band. Lubricant was replaced with ISO VG 46 turbine oil, and the oil condition was confirmed free of water and particulates.

### Verification
Post-reinstatement vibration was measured at 2.9 mm/s RMS (within limits). Bearing temperature stabilized at 71 °C after 30 minutes of operation (within the 85 °C limit, deviation from baseline within 15 °C). Hot alignment was verified at 0.04 mm at the coupling face.

### Approval
Pump P-199 was approved for reinstatement following the vibration exceedance event on 2026-03-12. Corrective action: bearing replacement. The equipment was released to operations on 2026-03-14 following satisfactory verification of all checklist items in Appendix A of SOP-042.
