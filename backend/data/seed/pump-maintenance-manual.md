# Pump Maintenance Manual — Bearing Inspection Chapter

**Equipment class:** Centrifugal pumps, API 610  **Revision:** 7

### Chapter 4 — Bearing Inspection and Condition Monitoring

4.1 Bearings shall be inspected at every planned maintenance interval and whenever vibration or temperature trends indicate degradation. The inspection records the bearing race condition, rolling element condition, cage integrity, and lubricant condition.

4.2 Acceptance criteria for reinstalled bearings: no scoring visible to the naked eye, no discoloration indicative of overheating, measured internal clearance within the manufacturer's tolerance band, and grease/oil condition free of particulate contamination or water ingress.

4.3 Bearing temperature must be recorded at startup and after 30 minutes of continuous operation. A deviation greater than 15 °C from baseline requires investigation of lubrication, alignment, and possible thrust loading.

4.4 When vibration at the bearing housing exceeds 4.5 mm/s RMS, the pump shall be taken out of service for bearing inspection. Repeated exceedance events on the same equipment trigger a root-cause analysis per MRPL-RCA-002.

4.5 All bearing replacements shall use bearings procured from the approved vendor list, with material certificates traceable to the heat number, and the replacement shall be logged in the equipment history card.
