# Inspection Standard IS-PMP-2024

## Acceptance Criteria for Rotating Equipment Reinstatement

### 3.1 General Acceptance Criteria
Acceptance of pump reinstatement requires documented verification of all checklist items in Appendix A, signed by the certifying engineer. No equipment shall be returned to service with an open critical checklist item.

### 3.2 Vibration Acceptance
For new and reinstalled centrifugal pumps, the vibration velocity measured at the bearing housing in the horizontal, vertical, and axial directions shall not exceed 4.5 mm/s RMS unfiltered. For equipment operating above 3000 RPM, the limit is reduced to 3.5 mm/s RMS.

### 3.3 Temperature Acceptance
Steady-state bearing temperatures measured at the outer race shall not exceed 85 °C for oil-lubricated and 95 °C for grease-lubricated bearings. A startup temperature excursion exceeding 15 °C above the established baseline is flagged for investigation.

### 3.4 Alignment Acceptance
Final hot alignment shall be verified with the machine at operating temperature. Misalignment shall not exceed 0.05 mm measured at the coupling face for flexible couplings, and 0.03 mm for gear couplings.

### 3.5 Documentation
Every reinstatement shall be accompanied by a completed checklist, a vibration spectrum at operating speed, a bearing temperature log, and a signed approval note from the certifying engineer before the equipment is released to operations.
